# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Sudo Security X is committed to providing an open, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in our projects and community.

We welcome people with different backgrounds, experience levels, technical interests, and perspectives.

Our community should be a place where people can **build, research, question, experiment, and learn** without harassment or intimidation.

---

## Expected Behavior

All contributors and community members are expected to:

- Treat others with respect.
- Communicate constructively.
- Give and receive technical criticism professionally.
- Assume good faith unless there is clear evidence otherwise.
- Credit the work of others.
- Respect intellectual property and licenses.
- Keep discussions focused and productive.
- Help newcomers learn and participate.
- Respect reasonable boundaries and privacy.
- Report security issues responsibly.

Disagreement is completely acceptable.

Personal attacks are not.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable within Sudo Security X projects or communities:

- Harassment or intimidation
- Personal attacks
- Threats of violence or harm
- Hate speech or discriminatory behavior
- Sexual harassment
- Deliberate humiliation or targeted abuse
- Doxxing or exposing private information
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Impersonation intended to deceive or harm
- Spam or disruptive behavior
- Malicious interference with community infrastructure
- Deliberately misleading contributors about security issues
- Unauthorized access to systems belonging to other community members

Security research does not provide an exception to these rules.

---

## Technical Disagreements

Technical disagreement is encouraged when it improves the work.

When disagreeing:

1. Address the idea rather than the person.
2. Explain your reasoning.
3. Provide evidence where possible.
4. Be willing to reconsider your position.
5. Keep criticism specific and constructive.

A strong technical argument is more valuable than an aggressive one.

---

## Security Research

Sudo Security X encourages responsible security research.

Community members must not use Sudo Security X infrastructure, repositories, discussions, or community spaces to:

- Attack unauthorized systems
- Steal credentials
- Expose private information
- Conduct disruptive attacks
- Distribute malicious software intended for harm
- Coordinate unauthorized intrusion
- Publish sensitive information obtained through illegal access

Research should be conducted within appropriate legal and ethical boundaries.

---

## Privacy

Respect the privacy of other contributors.

Do not publish or distribute:

- Private addresses
- Phone numbers
- Passwords
- Authentication credentials
- Private messages
- Personal documents
- Private account information
- Other sensitive personal information

without appropriate permission.

---

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it to the Sudo Security X maintainers.

For security vulnerabilities affecting Sudo Security X infrastructure, follow the instructions in:

`SECURITY.md`

For general community conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through the official Sudo Security X communication channels.

When reporting an incident, provide:

- What happened
- Where it happened
- When it happened
- Relevant links or evidence
- Any immediate concerns about safety or privacy

Please avoid publicly posting sensitive personal information.

---

## Enforcement

Sudo Security X maintainers may take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Possible actions include:

1. Informal warning
2. Request to modify or remove content
3. Temporary restriction from community participation
4. Removal from a project
5. Permanent removal from community spaces
6. Other appropriate action depending on the severity of the situation

Actions will be based on the circumstances and available evidence.

---

## Good-Faith Reports

Reports made honestly and in good faith will be taken seriously.

Retaliation against someone who reports a genuine concern is not acceptable.

False reports deliberately intended to harm another person may themselves constitute a violation of this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- Sudo Security X repositories
- Pull requests
- Issues
- Discussions
- Official communication channels
- Official community spaces
- Events organized by Sudo Security X
- Other spaces where someone is explicitly representing Sudo Security X

---

## Maintainer Responsibility

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Maintaining a respectful environment
- Reviewing conduct reports
- Protecting contributors from harassment
- Applying community rules consistently
- Handling sensitive information appropriately
- Escalating serious issues when necessary

Maintainers are expected to act fairly and in good faith.

---

## Final Principle

Sudo Security X exists to bring people together around technical work.

We can disagree.

We can challenge each other's ideas.

We can fail.

We can experiment.

We can learn.

But we should always leave the work, the discussion, and the community **better than we found them**.
