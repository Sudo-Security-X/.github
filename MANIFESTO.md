# The Sudo Security X Manifesto

> **Build. Research. Analyze. Publish.**

Technology is moving faster than most people can understand it.

Systems become more complex.

Artificial intelligence changes how software is built.

Security threats evolve continuously.

Privacy becomes harder to protect.

We believe the answer is not to simply consume technology.

It is to **understand it**.

---

## We Build

We do not want to be spectators.

We create tools, experiments, software, frameworks, and systems that help us understand and solve technical problems.

A working prototype is often more valuable than an idea that never leaves a document.

---

## We Research

We question assumptions.

We investigate systems.

We test hypotheses.

We analyze evidence.

We document both successful and unsuccessful experiments.

We do not treat speculation as fact.

Good research does not begin with certainty.

It begins with a question.

---

## We Analyze

Technology does not exist in isolation.

A vulnerability can affect organizations.

An AI system can introduce new security problems.

A software design decision can create consequences years later.

We study the technical, security, and societal implications of emerging technology.

---

## We Publish

Knowledge becomes more useful when it can be shared.

Whenever responsible, we publish:

- Source code
- Research
- Technical reports
- Experiments
- Writeups
- Documentation
- Methodologies
- Lessons learned

We prefer reproducible work over unsupported claims.

---

## We Question

"That's how it has always been done" is not a security model.

We question:

- Systems
- Assumptions
- Architectures
- Security practices
- Technologies
- Our own conclusions

Questioning something does not mean rejecting it.

It means understanding it.

---

## We Learn in Public

Not every experiment will succeed.

Not every hypothesis will be correct.

Not every project will become production software.

That's okay.

Failures can contain valuable information.

We document what went wrong, what we learned, and what we would do differently.

---

## We Build for Understanding

We do not build tools simply to make a repository larger.

We build because creating something forces us to understand the problem.

A tool should have a purpose.

A research project should have a question.

A writeup should have something worth learning.

---

## We Practice Responsible Security

Security research requires responsibility.

We support:

- Authorized testing
- Defensive research
- Responsible disclosure
- Controlled experimentation
- Privacy-respecting investigation
- Legal security research

Curiosity is not authorization.

Access is not permission.

Technical capability does not justify misuse.

---

## We Value the Work

Sudo Security X is not built around individual fame.

Contributors may work under their real identity or a pseudonym.

Recognition is welcome.

But the quality of the work matters more.

A well-researched report from an unknown contributor is more valuable than an impressive title without substance.

---

## We Share Knowledge

We believe technical knowledge should be accessible.

Documentation should be understandable.

Research should explain its methodology.

Tools should be documented.

When possible, we build openly so that others can learn, improve, challenge, and extend the work.

---

## We Stay Curious

Cybersecurity changes.

Artificial intelligence changes.

Technology changes.

The problems we study today may look completely different tomorrow.

So we keep learning.

We keep experimenting.

We keep asking questions.

---
